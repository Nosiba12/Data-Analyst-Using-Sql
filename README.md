# Data-Analysis-Using-Sql
Swiggy Data Analysis: Insights on Food Orders Across the Platform

📊 Overview

A comprehensive analysis of Swiggy data was conducted to understand order patterns and restaurant performance, and coustomer preferences 
and creating multidimensional data model.

🔍 Main steps of the analysis:

1. Data Cleaning

· ✅ Checking for empty values and missing data
· ✅ Detecting and removing duplicate records (341 duplicate records removed)
· ✅ Handling inconsistent data

2. Data Engineering

Five dimension tables and one fact table were created:

· Dimension tables: Date, Location, Restaurant, Rating, Dish
· Fact table: Order requests with performance metrics.

📈 Key Results

🎯 Key Performance Indicators

· Total Orders: A comprehensive analysis of all orders
· Total Revenue: Measured in millions of rupees
· Average Dish Price: For price comparison
· Average Ratings: To measure customer satisfaction

🔄 Order Trends

· Monthly Trends: Identifying peak and low months
· Annual Growth: Tracking order growth over the years
· Daily Patterns: Identifying the most active days of the week

🏙️ Geographic Distribution

· Top 10 Cities: By order volume
· State Contribution: To total revenue

🍽️ Restaurant and Dish Performance

· Top 10 Restaurants: By number of orders
· Most Common Categories: For cuisines
· Most Ordered Dishes: Customer preferences

💰 Spending Analysis

· Price Segments: Order distribution by price ranges
· Kitchen Performance: Evaluation based on orders and ratings

⭐ Ratings Analysis

· Rating Distribution and Understanding Customer Rating Criteria

🎨 Conclusions:

This analysis provides valuable insights for:

· Swiggy management: to improve operations and planning
· Restaurants: to understand their performance and enhance their offerings
· Customers: to understand market trends

The data model is designed to be scalable and easy for future queries, while ensuring data quality and accuracy through a comprehensive cleaning process.
